### 🎵 Songs Page — Track-Level Deep Dive
![Songs Page - Default View](<img width="1115" height="622" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-17 010306" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a51af0a9-1405-4fb4-b95a-7de4837e1c72" />

A searchable song list on the left drives all visuals on the right. Selecting a track shows its **average popularity**, **first release date**, a **popularity breakdown by song**, and a **12-month popularity trend**.

![Songs Page - Filtered by Song](<img width="1114" height="621" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-17 010320" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/85b15e9c-ab71-4639-9e6a-d4c5643059b5" />

Example: selecting *"Slut!" (Taylor's Version)* by Taylor Swift filters every visual to show that track's specific popularity (85.76), release date (2023-10-26), and its popularity trend across the months it charted.
